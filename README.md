# VORTEX 🌪️

### Intelligent Financial Literacy & Decision-Support Platform

> **Understand your money. Protect your financial stability. Plan your goals. Decide with confidence.**

VORTEX is an intelligent financial-literacy and decision-support platform designed to help people understand, manage, and improve their financial decisions.

It transforms complex financial information into clear, personalized insights—helping users understand where their money goes, protect their financial stability, plan meaningful goals, and make informed decisions before they spend.

---

##  The Idea

Managing money is not only about tracking expenses.

A person may know:

- how much money they earn,
- how much they spend,
- and how much they have saved,

but still not know:

> **"Am I actually financially okay?"**

VORTEX is designed around this problem.

Instead of simply telling users to spend less, VORTEX helps them understand the **impact of their financial decisions**.
